About the presets

“1920p” clarification: this app interprets that label as 1920 × 1080 (1080p / Full HD). “720p” is 1280 × 720. UHD is 3840 × 2160. The custom tall test is exactly 1080 × 8000.

Each click creates a different random abstract composition. The browser canvas is used to draw the image and encode it as PNG. UHD and tall images contain 8.29 million and 8.64 million pixels respectively; exporting them may take longer and temporarily use more memory.
